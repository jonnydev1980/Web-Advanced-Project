Who We Are

A Passion Project Turned
Global Agency

Looking & Booking was born from a simple belief: travel should be effortless, inspiring, and accessible to everyone. What started as a small team of travel enthusiasts in Belgrade has grown into a full-service booking agency trusted by over 250,000 travelers in 60+ countries.

We partner directly with airlines, hotel groups, and tour operators to give you access to prices and packages you simply won't find elsewhere. Our expert team of travel consultants is available 24/7 to craft your perfect journey — from weekend city breaks to month-long adventures.
