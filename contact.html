Common Questions

How quickly will I receive my booking confirmation?

Instantly for most flights and hotels. For custom packages, our team will confirm within 2 hours during business hours.

Can I modify or cancel my booking?

Yes. Most bookings allow free changes up to 48h before departure. Contact our support team and we'll take care of it.

Do you offer group travel bookings?

Absolutely! We specialize in group bookings of 10+ travelers with exclusive group rates and dedicated support.

Is my payment secure?

100%. We use bank-level SSL encryption and are PCI-DSS compliant. Your financial data is never stored.
